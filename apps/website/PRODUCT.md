# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and operators of physical spaces who want those spaces to be experienceable
online before a visitor ever shows up: hotels, holiday rentals, restaurants and
cafes, real estate, event venues, retail and showrooms. They are not technical.
They evaluate quickly, mostly on a phone (70%+ of traffic is mobile), and decide
whether a tour is worth a conversation within the first few seconds. The job they
are hiring StepInside for: "show my space more convincingly than photos can, so
the right people reach out."

## Product Purpose

StepInside produces photorealistic, freely walkable 3D tours of interiors and
outdoor areas. A visitor opens a link and moves through the space on any device,
with no app download. The site sells a complete per-object package (walkable scan,
information hotspots, a guided camera path, an embed code, a print-ready QR code,
and an embedding guide for the customer's web designer). The website's job is to
prove the experience in seconds and convert interest into a contact (form, email,
phone, or a Calendly call). Price is never shown. Success = the hero plus the first
scan convince fast, and the visitor reaches the contact section.

## Brand Personality

Clear and direct, no marketing-speak. Confident, not arrogant. Results-oriented:
it talks about what the customer gets, not the technology behind it. Calm and
professional, like infrastructure that does not need to perform. The "About" voice
is first-person plural (Wir-Form). Three words: measured, credible, quietly
confident.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / startup landing pages (gradient hero, feature-card triptychs,
  "next-gen / disruptive / AI-powered" copy).
- Any mention of the underlying technology: never say AI/KI, Gaussian Splatting,
  or Matterport; never "better than Matterport".
- Buzzwords and jargon of any kind.
- Stock photography, lifestyle imagery, human subjects, decorative stock.
- Shadow-, gradient-, and bright-accent-heavy "designed" looks.
- Animation overload. Motion stays subtle and purposeful.
- A blog, a chat widget, cookie-banner overload, or a visible price.

## Design Principles

1. **The scans are the star.** Everything else recedes so the walkable tours carry
   the page. The site is a frame around the real content, not a show in itself.
2. **Show the result, not the technology.** Describe what the customer receives and
   what their visitors experience. Never expose the machinery.
3. **Quiet confidence over performance.** An engineering dossier, not a sales pitch.
   Restraint reads as competence.
4. **Mobile-first and fast.** 70%+ mobile; the hero and first scan must convince in
   about three seconds. No heavy assets.
5. **Tonal restraint builds trust.** A warm-neutral, near-monochromatic system with
   exactly one dramatic dark inversion; contrast comes from tone, not colour.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (body text >= 4.5:1; large text >= 3:1; placeholders >= 4.5:1).
`prefers-reduced-motion` is honored everywhere (reveals collapse to static).
Semantic headings (one h1, one h2 per section), real labels on every form input,
and visible keyboard focus states on all interactive elements. The site is
bilingual: German is primary, English is an option; the brand name "StepInside"
always stays in English.
